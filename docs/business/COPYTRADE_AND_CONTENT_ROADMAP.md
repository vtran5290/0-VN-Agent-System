## Content rules

### Allowed (education-first)

- Market regime commentary
- Watchlist education (process, not calls)
- Risk management frameworks
- Trade lessons (with losses shown)
- Weekly review transparency
- Process and stage-gate updates

### Avoid

- “Buy now” / “sell now” urgency
- Guaranteed returns
- Pumpy stock calls
- Hiding losses or drawdowns
- Auto-posting ticker-specific calls
- Taking money or account access from followers

### Human approval

All public posts with tickers require human approval before publish. Use `templates/content/compliance_safe_post_template.md`.

## Compliance posture

- Separate **facts** vs **interpretation**
- No investment advice without licensed structure
- No managing third-party accounts
- Referral/copytrade only after broker route confirmed
