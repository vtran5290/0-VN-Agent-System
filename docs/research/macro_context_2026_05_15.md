## 3. Inflation and oil channel

### Facts
- Brent average April 2026: **USD 102.46/bbl**
- Brent peak referenced: **USD 119.5/bbl**
- Domestic petroleum average April 2026: **+44.2% YoY**
- RON95: **+21% YoY**; diesel: **+60.8% YoY**
- CPI April 2026: **5.46% YoY**; core CPI: **4.66% YoY**

### Transmission channel (factual sequence; not a policy prediction)
Oil price → domestic fuel prices → transport costs → goods/services prices → CPI/core CPI pressure → **monetary policy constraint** (channel described in MBS macro framing; SBV reaction path not specified as committed action in the numbers above).

### Derived calculation
- **Derived:** Headline CPI (5.46%) exceeded core CPI (4.66%) by **0.80 percentage points** in April 2026 (arithmetic from MBS figures).

### Interpretation to monitor
- **Interpretation to monitor:** If Brent stays near or above USD 100/bbl, domestic fuel YoY increases already observed may continue to feed transport and goods/services components unless domestic fuel adjustment cadence or global oil prices change.

## 5. Liquidity and interest-rate channel

### Facts
- SBV banking meeting: **9 April 2026**
- Post-guidance deposit rate cuts: **0.2–0.5 pp**, mainly tenors **≥6 months**
- End-April average 12M deposit: **8.31%** (all), **8.16%** (tier-1), **8.45%** (tier-2)
- vs beginning of 2026: **+2.53 pp** on average 12M deposit rate
- Overnight interbank: **9.3%** (end-Mar) → **3.75%** (24 Apr) → **6.3%** (end-Apr)
- April OMO: inject **350.1tn**, mature **330.6tn**, net **+19.5tn**; outstanding **309.6tn**

### Factual description
Deposit-rate cuts occurred **after** SBV policy guidance in April, while **level** of 12M deposit rates remained **materially higher** than at the start of 2026 (+2.53 pp). Interbank overnight rates **fell sharply** mid-April then **rose** into month-end.

### Interpretation to monitor
- **Interpretation to monitor:** MBS year-end 12M deposit forecast (6.0–6.5% for six large banks) implies further decline from end-April 8.31% average if the forecast is realized — path and timing not specified in the snapshot table.

## 6. Open questions to monitor

1. Whether CPI and core CPI moderate after Q2 (vs April 5.46% / 4.66%).
2. Whether Brent remains above USD 100/bbl (April average 102.46; peak referenced 119.5).
3. Whether USD/VND stays within MBS Q2 forecast range **26,350–26,700**.
4. Whether the 4M trade deficit reverses toward MBS full-year trade surplus forecast (~USD 23bn).
5. Whether deposit rates continue declining after April cuts (vs +2.53 pp vs year-start).
6. Whether interbank rates stabilize below April-end 6.3% (vs 3.75% on 24 April).
7. Whether public investment disbursement accelerates after April (4M completion 14.1% of plan).
8. Whether real estate bond refinancing proceeds smoothly through the rest of Q2 (see bond context note).
9. Whether high-coupon real estate bond issuers show project-level cash collection (issuer-level; no aggregate cash metric in MBS macro).
10. Whether delayed bond payment ratio remains near 2.3% or worsens (bond report).
11. Whether CTCK DCM/IB revenue reflects corporate bond issuance recovery (channel mapping only; no revenue data in MBS macro).
12. Whether AI/electronics export growth continues after strong 4M2026 (+49.1% YoY segment).
