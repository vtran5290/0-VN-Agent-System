## What NOT To Do

**Do not do any of the following before or during Year 1:**

### Structural
- Do not register an LLC or any legal entity — public records break anonymity
- Do not set up payment rails (Stripe, bank transfer, any subscription billing)
- Do not deploy Streamlit dashboard publicly
- Do not publish paper-trade NAV, P&L, win rate, or any performance number
- Do not publish allocation JSON or any `{ticker: weight}` schema
- Do not publish the execution audit log

### Content
- Do not name individual stocks with directional language ("FPT is forming a base")
- Do not use: buy, sell, hold, signal, alert, stop, target, portfolio, allocation, model portfolio
- Do not use: "if X, do Y" as a trading rule — historical-observation framing only
- Do not publish case studies using current tickers or signals from the last 6 months
- Do not quote performance: no %, no returns, no "my model returned..."
- Do not disclose that the system runs paper trades or that you have simulated returns

### Identity
- Do not post from personal social accounts
- Do not cross-link anonymous account to personal identity
- Do not use work device or work Wi-Fi for any anonymous-account activity
- Do not work on public content during work hours if the contract restricts it
- Do not use your existing writing-style vocabulary on the anonymous account

### Operations
- Do not self-host an email list — use Substack/Beehiiv for PDPD compliance
- Do not OAUTH-link anonymous accounts to personal Google/GitHub
- Do not skip WHOIS privacy on the domain (must be enabled from day 1)
