# Language Sanitization Map
**Date:** 2026-06-17
**Purpose:** Before/after replacement table for all risky language in public content
**Council authority:** Opus judgment gate (2026-06-17)

> Wording sanitization helps but is not a legal shield. A Vietnamese regulator looks at function, not labels. This map reduces surface-level risk and establishes consistent research-notebook voice — it does not make the project legally cleared. Qualified legal consult required before paid launch.

## Why Each Category Is Dangerous

### Category A — Direct action words
**Risk:** These words tell a reader what to do with a specific security. Under VN Law on Securities 2019, Art. 74 and Decree 155/2020, advising someone to buy, sell, or hold a specific security without an investment consultancy license is the core prohibited activity. Even one imperative sentence ("Buy FPT") can characterize the entire publication as investment advice.

### Category B — Signal / alert language
**Risk:** "Signal" and "alert" imply actionable, time-sensitive information derived from a system. This maps directly to the definition of investment recommendation / phân tích đầu tư. A free signal is still a signal. Courts and regulators look at whether a reasonable retail investor would act on the output — not whether you called it a "signal" or a "research note."

### Category C — Portfolio / position language
**Risk:** Words like "portfolio," "allocation," "position," "model portfolio," and "sizing" describe portfolio construction and management activity — a separately licensed function in VN (portfolio management / quản lý danh mục). Publishing allocation weights or position sizes with tickers is the equivalent of operating a fund without a license, regardless of disclaimers.

### Category D — Price target / stop language
**Risk:** Target prices and stop levels are the core deliverable of licensed sell-side research (phân tích đầu tư). Attaching a price or level to a recommendation is what distinguishes a research report from a news article in regulatory terms.

### Category E — Personalized / prescriptive framing
**Risk:** "You should," "if you hold," "for your portfolio" converts a general observation into personalized financial advice — which requires a separately licensed advisory relationship in VN.

### Category F — Performance / track record language
**Risk:** Quoting a return number — even from a paper account — creates a performance track record implication. Track records are associated with licensed portfolio management and investment advisory. Even "my model returned X%" can be read as a solicitation by a regulator.

| **hold** | Portfolio management instruction | "continues to meet the baseline criteria" |

| **trim** | Portfolio management instruction | "screen weighting reduced by rules" |

## Structural Sentence Patterns to Replace

### Before (advice pattern):
> "FPT is forming a base above the 21-week EMA and could be ready to break out. I'm watching it closely."

### After (observation pattern):
> "Among stocks currently meeting the EMA-cloud criterion, the industrial software subsector shows a concentration of qualifying names. This is an objective observation about the screen output — not a view on any individual stock."

---

### Before (signal pattern):
> "Signal fired on VHM today. Entry zone: 45,000–46,000. Stop: 42,500."

### After (criterion pattern):
> "The momentum screen flagged an updated criterion match in the real estate sector this session. Historical reference levels for this criterion type (from 2012–2024 backtest data) have shown X% of cases resolved within N weeks. This is a historical observation only."

---

### Before (performance pattern):
> "My model is up 34% YTD vs VNINDEX +12%. Here's what it's holding."

### After (never — block entirely):
> [This sentence and all variants must not appear in public content.]

---

### Before (action board pattern):
> "A3 board: Add VIC (regime bull, RS rank 12). Exit HPG (downtrend probability >70%)."

### After (observation pattern):
> "The rules-based framework this week: [regime label]. Breadth condition: [count above EMA]. RS leadership: concentrated in [sector]. Historical context for similar regimes: [description]. This is a rules-based framework observation — not a trading plan."
