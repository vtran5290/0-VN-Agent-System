## WHAT THIS IS

```
This is a public notebook documenting a systematic, rules-based approach
to observing Vietnam equity markets.

The notebook covers:

— Market regime: A rules-based classification framework (bull / bear / fragile)
  derived from price, breadth, and trend data across the VNINDEX and the
  broader stock universe.

— Breadth conditions: How broadly market participation is distributed —
  what percentage of stocks are above key trend criteria at any given time.

— Relative strength: How individual stocks and sectors rank against each
  other and against the index — which areas are leading and which are lagging.

— Rules-based framework behavior: How the systematic framework has behaved
  in historical Vietnamese market conditions (2012–present). What patterns
  emerge. What the framework's limits are.

— Macro context: How global macro conditions (USD, US Treasuries, global
  liquidity) interact with Vietnam-specific dynamics.

All observations are derived from public market data. All methodology is
described explicitly so readers can evaluate the reasoning.
```
