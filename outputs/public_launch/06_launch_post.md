**What Quan Trắc is**

A weekly market observation notebook, published anonymously.

The framework covers roughly 272 liquid Vietnam stocks from 2012 to the present.
Every week, it produces a structured set of observations:

- Market regime — a rules-based classification (bull, bear, or fragile) derived
  from breadth, trend, and price criteria
- Breadth conditions — how broadly market participation is distributed
- Relative strength — which sectors and stocks rank highest by the framework's criteria
- Macro context — how global and Vietnam-specific macro conditions interact
- Framework behavior notes — how the rules-based system behaved this week,
  and what its known limitations are
- Historical case studies — a look at a similar past market period,
  always at least six months old

The methodology is derived from published systematic frameworks — O'Neil,
Minervini, technical breadth analysis — adapted to Vietnam market structure
and calibrated on historical data since 2012.
