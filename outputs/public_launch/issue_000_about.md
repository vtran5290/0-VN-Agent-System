## What is Quan Trắc?

Quan Trắc is a weekly observation notebook on the Vietnamese equity market — HOSE, HNX, and UPCOM.

Every week, the same rules-based framework is applied to the same data, and the results are documented. What changed. What the model observed. What risks are being monitored. No tips. No targets. No signals.

The name "Quan Trắc" means observation or monitoring in Vietnamese — the same word used for environmental monitoring stations. That framing is deliberate: this is a measurement instrument, not an opinion column.

---

## What the framework measures

Each weekly issue covers the same structure:

1. **Regime** — Is the market in a bull, bear, or fragile state? The classification is rules-based, driven by two inputs: global liquidity conditions and Vietnam domestic liquidity conditions. The regime determines the framework's overall posture.

2. **Breadth** — How many stocks are participating in the market's direction? Distribution day counts, trend-vs-moving-average readings across VN30, HNX, and UPCOM. Breadth divergence — when large-caps weaken but mid/small-caps hold — is flagged explicitly.

3. **Relative strength** — Cross-sectional ranking of ~272 liquid Vietnamese stocks by benchmark-relative performance. Derived from O'Neil/Minervini methodology, adapted for Vietnam market conditions. Sector-level observations only — individual stock names are not published.

4. **Macro context** — US Treasury yields, DXY, Vietnam interbank rates, SBV OMO activity, credit growth. All sourced from FRED and SBV public data.

5. **Downtrend probability** — A rules-based analog model that compares current VNINDEX conditions to historical instances. Outputs probability estimates with explicit sample sizes, methodology notes, and confidence caveats.

6. **Historical case studies** — Each issue includes one historical case study (≥6 months old) illustrating how the framework behaved during a comparable market period. These are structural observations, not forecasts.

## The methodology in brief

The framework uses a fixed, rules-based approach applied without discretion:

- **Regime classification** combines global liquidity (UST yields, DXY, Fed policy stance) with Vietnam domestic liquidity (SBV interbank rate, OMO, credit growth) to determine one of three states: Bull, Bear, or Fragile.

- **Relative strength scoring** ranks stocks by their performance relative to VNINDEX using weighted return-over-cash measures across multiple timeframes (21d, 63d, 126d, 189d, 252d). This is a cross-sectional ranking, not a signal.

- **Distribution day counting** tracks institutional supply days on VN30, HNX, and UPCOM using volume + price-decline criteria. Elevated counts (5+) tighten the framework's posture.

- **Downtrend probability** uses k-nearest-neighbor analog matching on price position relative to the 50-day and 20-day moving averages. Probabilities are shrinkage-adjusted against baseline rates and walk-forward calibrated. Sample sizes are always disclosed.

All data draws from public sources: OHLCV from market data providers, macro from FRED and SBV. No proprietary or privileged data is used.

## How to read the memo

Each issue follows the same 10-section structure. Readers should:

- **Read the regime and breadth sections first** — they set the context for everything else.
- **Treat probability numbers as base rates, not predictions** — they describe what happened historically in similar conditions, not what will happen this time.
- **Note the caveats** — every data point includes its source, its date, and its limitations.
- **Do your own work** — this notebook provides observations and framework behavior. Investment decisions require analysis, suitability assessment, and risk management that no newsletter can provide.
