What this is

A weekly market observation memo

Every week, the notebook publishes a structured set of observations derived from a rules-based framework applied to ~272 liquid Vietnam stocks tracked from 2012 to the present.

  • Regime reading — a rules-based classification (bull, bear, or fragile) derived from breadth, trend, and price criteria across the VNINDEX and the broader stock universe
  • Breadth conditions — how broadly market participation is distributed; what percentage of stocks meet key trend criteria at any given time
  • Relative strength landscape — which sectors and areas currently rank highest by objective rules-based criteria, and which are lagging
  • Framework behavior notes — how the systematic framework behaved this week, including its known limitations
  • Historical case studies — observations from similar past market periods, presented as structural context, not prediction
  • Macro context — how global conditions (USD, US Treasuries) interact with Vietnam-specific dynamics (SBV, interbank, credit)
