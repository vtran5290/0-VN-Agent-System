## Regime Verdict (current)

**Growth-forcing policy regime:** bullish for capital formation, banks, market infrastructure, power/grid, construction and rental housing; constrained by CPI, FX, foreign-flow and bank funding risk.

This is **not broad monetary easing**. It is **selective credit allocation** and **fiscal/project acceleration** under a 10%+ GDP ambition.

**Market overlay (unchanged from Jun 12 tape):** VNI bear tactical gate still active below 1,810 until reclaimed. Policy regime is **context for sector selection**, not an automatic override of defensive market posture.

### INFERENCE — What Resolution 168 changes for PM7

1. **Policy impulse shifts from “defend target rhetorically” to “operational growth-forcing toolkit.”** Fiscal surplus deployment, MT public investment allocation, disbursement scenario tracking, and treasury–bank coordination are actionable levers.
2. **Credit is selective, not blanket easing.** Production, priority sectors, national projects, and rental/social housing funding are the stated channels — aligns with quality/state banks and project-finance names over aggressive LDR stretch banks.
3. **First-order equity beneficiaries (directness + policy fit):** banks (project/priority credit), securities/capital-market intermediaries, public-investment contractors, construction materials, power/grid, Vingroup/TOD-linked infrastructure champions.
4. **Second-order beneficiaries:** KCN/FDI, logistics, tourism/retail, residential/rental housing with explicit policy alignment — **not all real estate**.
5. **Constraint layer dominates execution:** CPI rebound, FX pressure, trade deficit, deposit-rate/funding stress, bank concentration, material price controls, foreign outflows, and policy execution slippage can invalidate the bullish policy read quickly.

### RISK LAYER (monitoring — not speculation)

| Risk | Mechanism | PM7 response |
|------|-----------|--------------|
| CPI rebound | Growth-forcing + salary step + energy/transport | Tighten bank/materials sizing; no “easing” narrative |
| FX pressure | Import surge + foreign outflow + growth push | Prefer exporters/FX-hedged cash flows; cut FX-sensitive banks |
| Trade deficit | Import-heavy growth mix | Favor local-content/industrial policy names vs pure import plays |
| Deposit / funding stress | Credit push without deposit formation | Favor treasury-deposit beneficiaries (BID/CTG/VCB) vs MBB/VPB/HDB |
| Bank concentration | Selective quota + LDR stress at aggressive banks | Quality bank barbell; caution bucket for high-LDR names |
| Material price controls | Anti-hoarding / license reclamation | Monitor margin squeeze at materials; order-book > price hope |
| Foreign outflows | Policy bullish but flow bearish | Require flow confirmation for adds |
| Execution risk | Disbursement scenarios ≠ actual spend | Weekly/monthly public investment tracking is the gate |
